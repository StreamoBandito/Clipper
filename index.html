Clipper

A private desktop app by MWP Films for the Streamo Bandito gaming channel.

Clipper helps one small team turn their own Grand Theft Auto gameplay into short videos for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook. It runs on the team's own Mac. It reads the channel's own YouTube statistics (views, likes, comments on its own videos) so the team can see which videos do well.

Clipper is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. It does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
